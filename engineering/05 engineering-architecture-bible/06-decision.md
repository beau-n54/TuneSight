# TuneSight Engineering Architecture Bible

## Engineering Domain 06 — Decision

## Engineering Intent and Purpose

Decision transforms authoritative engineering understanding into qualified guidance. It assists engineering judgement and shall never replace it.

## Authoritative Ownership

Decision solely owns recommendations, action priorities, alternatives, decision risk, guidance confidence and recommendation rationale.

## Responsibilities

- Produce and prioritise supported recommendations.
- Identify alternatives, risks and required human judgement.
- Distinguish guidance from fact.
- Publish traceable qualified actions.

## Boundaries

Decision shall not establish identity, Knowledge, Evidence, Correlation or Explanation; perform root-cause reasoning; preserve history; or control Presentation.

## Primary Dependencies

Decision primarily consumes authoritative Explanation.

## Traceability Dependencies

Decision may consult preserved qualified Identity, Knowledge, Evidence and Correlation references when required for safety, scope, confidence, risk or traceability. This does not authorise reconstruction or requalification of upstream truth.

## Outputs and Consumers

It publishes recommendations, priorities, alternatives, risks, rationale, confidence and unknowns. Memory, Evolution and Presentation may consume these without redefining them.

## Contract Obligations

Outputs follow the [Cross-Domain Engineering Contracts](00-cross-domain-engineering-contracts.md). Guidance shall identify the authoritative understanding on which it depends and preserve all supplied qualifications.

## Engineering Rules

- Recommendations are not engineering facts.
- Guidance requires authoritative explanation appropriate to the risk.
- Confidence is not permission or verification.
- Human engineering judgement remains explicit.
- Unsupported action remains withheld.

## Failure Behaviour

Where reliable guidance cannot be established, Decision exposes uncertainty, alternatives or no recommendation. It shall never produce action merely to complete a workflow.

## Validation and Evolution

Validation shall assess traceability, risk communication, repeatability and real-world outcomes. Guidance methods may evolve without rewriting the historical truth or reasoning they consumed.

## Closing Responsibility

Decision is responsible for authoritative engineering guidance that supports, but never replaces, human judgement.
