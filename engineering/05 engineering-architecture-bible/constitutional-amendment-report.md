# Constitutional Amendment Report

**Subject:** Complete consolidation of the Engineering Architecture Bible

**Status:** Submitted for Founder Review
**Governance effect:** None until Founder ratification is separately recorded

## Constitutional Audit

The audit compared the Bible with the Manifesto, Constitution, Principles, Blueprint, Production Contract, governance records, status and register material, ratified Work Packages, amendments and established terminology.

It identified repeated generic language across domain chapters, inconsistent qualification vocabulary, weak cross-references, implicit contract obligations and one material classification conflict: Presentation was called Domain 09 although the Blueprint defines Presentation as a non-domain layer.

## Chapter Amendment Record

- `README.md` was completely rewritten as the constitutional entry point. It adds authority order, eight-domain organisation, Presentation classification, universal invariants, amendment rules and navigation.
- `00-cross-domain-engineering-contracts.md` is new. It formalises contracts as a universal architectural standard without creating a Domain, runtime layer, framework or implementation interface.
- `01-vehicle-identity.md` was rewritten to clarify current-system identity ownership, Knowledge dependency and exact-evidence obligations.
- `02-knowledge.md` was rewritten and consolidated. Reusable Engineering Truth, Engineering Context, first-class Relationships, Stock Variants, qualification, provenance, conflict, lifecycle and acquisition remain preserved.
- `03-evidence.md` was rewritten to separate observation from interpretation.
- `04-correlation.md` was rewritten to separate analysis relationships from Knowledge Relationships and association from causation.
- `05-explanation.md` was rewritten to identify the first reasoning authority and preserve competing explanations.
- `06-decision.md` was rewritten to distinguish guidance from fact and preserve human judgement.
- `07-memory.md` was rewritten to distinguish historical preservation from canonical reusable Knowledge.
- `08-evolution.md` was rewritten to distinguish improvement proposals from authority and ratification.
- `09-presentation.md` was rewritten as an architectural layer. Its communication responsibility remains; its former Domain classification was removed.

## Duplication Removed

Repeated statements about sole ownership, consumer prohibition, uncertainty, failure and evolution were consolidated into the universal contract standard and retained locally only where domain-specific meaning is required.

## Terminology Improved

The rewrite consistently distinguishes authority, evidence, verification, confidence, provenance, unknown, conflict, qualification, ownership, dependency, consumer, contract and implementation interface.

“Engineering Relationships” now distinguishes canonical reusable Knowledge Relationships from analysis-specific Correlation.

## Cross-References Added

Every chapter references the Cross-Domain Engineering Contracts. The README links every architectural chapter. Vehicle Identity and Knowledge cross-reference their complementary ownership boundary.

## Inconsistencies Resolved

- Presentation is no longer described as an Engineering Domain.
- Circular collaboration no longer implies circular ownership.
- Persistence, routes, workflows and interfaces are explicitly denied engineering authority.
- Confidence can no longer be read as verification.
- Workflow success can no longer be read as evidence.
- Evolution proposals can no longer be read as self-ratifying truth.

## Founder Decisions Preserved

The eight established Domain responsibilities, lifecycle order, sole-owner principle, Knowledge/Vehicle Identity boundary, Presentation non-authority, evidence-first philosophy, honest unknown state, WP-003 Stock Variant finding and governance authority are preserved.

## Assumptions Left Unchanged

No domain was added or removed. No ownership was transferred. No runtime design, schema, API, type, test, Work Package, Production Contract rule or governance record was changed.

## Recommendations Intentionally Not Implemented

No implementation contract registry, shared runtime framework, schema standard, API convention, migration, generated type workflow, test harness, deployment control or new governance state was introduced. These would require separate authorised work.

## Ratification Position

This rewrite is a consolidated draft. Existing ratified decisions remain authoritative until Founder review approves this text and Governance records ratification or supersession.
