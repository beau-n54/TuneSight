# WP-004.3.19 — BMW Calibration Applicability Evidence-Package Factory

Status: implemented and validated; candidate Evidence only; no new applicability accepted or published

Authority boundary: the factory assembles immutable technical and provenance Evidence for governed review. Readiness is not applicability authority. This work creates no decision, publication instruction, qualified relationship, Stock upgrade, semantic Knowledge, Workshop selector, or runtime family support.

## Package contract

`tunesight.calibration-applicability-evidence-package.v1` binds an exact XDF Source Artifact and digest, Definition Set identity and revision, source path/family/status, claimed identities, exact candidate binary digests and containers, identity observations, structural census, bounded extraction and conversion audits, Stock Variant responses, provenance, typed conflicts, limitations, and downstream proposal contract versions.

Package identity binds the Definition Set and sorted exact binary digests. Package revision binds the complete material dossier, including source and binary provenance and authority inputs. Canonical hashing, deterministic ordering, and deep freezing make equal inputs repeatable and material changes observable. Construction fails safely when the supplied Source Artifact, Definitions, and Definition Set do not bind.

The factory reuses the existing ROM identity observation, binary Evidence, applicability proposal/assessment, raw extraction, safe equation conversion, and Stock Variant response contracts. Every embedded proposal remains `pending`; `publicationSideEffects` is always empty.

## Evidence and conflict model

The complete source census preserves totals and unique revisions; duplicate-revision occurrence groups; scalar, 1D, and 2D counts; descriptions, categories, units, axes, and equations; extraction/conversion eligibility; representation conflicts; and representative blockers. Source paths and filenames remain provenance hints, never authority.

Each binary retains digest, container, byte length, role, independent provenance and Stock response, all configured credible marker observations with raw representation, normalized form, encoding, offsets and occurrence count, candidate-specific/common/contradictory classification, ambiguity, structural correspondence, and bounded extraction/conversion results.

Conflicts remain separately typed as identity, platform/family, Stock Variant, representation, equation/unit, source authority, duplicate-source ambiguity, and binary-candidate ambiguity. They are not collapsed into a generic warning.

## Readiness outcomes

- `ready_for_authority_review`: technically sufficient and established source authority, but still no acceptance.
- `more_evidence_required`: no hard contradiction, but required technical Evidence is absent.
- `technical_conflict`: a material technical contradiction exists.
- `source_authority_unresolved`: technical correspondence may be strong, but provenance/authority is insufficient.
- `no_responsible_candidate`: no candidate binary was responsibly supplied.
- `invalid`: reserved for safely failed package construction.

## Responsible batch architecture

The deterministic batch accepts explicit responsible candidate jobs or explicit prefilter rejections, orders by job identity, and never creates an uncontrolled XDF-by-binary Cartesian product. A prefilter rejection may record qualified impossibility Evidence such as container, byte-length, ECU-family, ROM-identity, or lineage contradiction. It cannot establish authority.

## N54 controls

The accepted current N54 cohort—I8A0S, IJE0S, IKM0S, and INA0S—reconstructed two-binary Original/MapSwitch dossiers with observed ROM markers and `ready_for_authority_review` readiness when the already-accepted source authority was supplied. This is a positive control only and creates no new N54 decision.

The IJE0S Definition Set paired with an INA0S Original binary produced `technical_conflict`, proving that a structurally attractive wrong-ROM pairing does not receive a reviewable dossier.

## S58 complete discovery

The complete repository census produced 10 source-bound packages, 12,556 Definitions, and 20 exact candidate binaries: one Original and one MapSwitch candidate for every source. All sources were structurally clean under the representation contract and had observable candidate-specific identity Evidence. All 10 outcomes were `source_authority_unresolved`; none was published. These ten paired cohorts are the strongest candidates for a later Founder-controlled authority review because they have complete co-located two-binary technical Evidence, while repository lineage and filenames remain insufficient authority.

## B58 Gen2 complete discovery

The complete distinct B58 Gen2 census produced 45 source-bound packages, 55,667 Definitions, and 121 exact candidate binaries. Forty-two packages with supplied responsible candidates were `source_authority_unresolved`; three sources without a responsible co-located candidate were `no_responsible_candidate`. Candidate-specific markers were observed in real candidates.

The configured common/shared MG1 marker `00003076501103` was not observed in any of the 121 real Gen2 candidates. The factory preserves that absence and does not manufacture common-marker ambiguity. Sources with several co-located candidates retain every exact binary separately; a common marker alone can never create an exact pairing. No B58 Gen2 relationship was accepted or published.

## B58 Gen1 compatibility proof

A bounded compatibility test uses the real `000030765A3C06` source and an MG1-shaped controlled binary containing both candidate-specific and common/shared encoded markers. The unchanged package types preserve marker multiplicity and ambiguity alongside the source's representation conflicts. Stock Evidence remains independently candidate-grade. This proves architectural fit for the known B58 Gen1 difficulties without commencing its admission programme.

## BMW-wide extensibility

The unchanged factory input and output types represented binary-absent examples from N55/S55/N13 lineage, N55E, S63, and B58 Gen1. N20 and future BMW cohorts use the same source, binary, marker, provenance, conflict, and readiness primitives; no family-specific core package type is required.

## Limitations and performance

Structural census covers every Definition. Per-binary raw extraction and engineering conversion are deterministically bounded to eight representative Definitions, prioritising representation conflicts and then stable Definition order. Packages explicitly disclose the attempted/success/blocked counts and never imply a complete extraction audit.

On the development host, the complete S58 batch took approximately 10.0 seconds and B58 Gen2 approximately 67.0 seconds. Timings are observations only and are excluded from package identity. No memoization was introduced.
