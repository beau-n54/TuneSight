# TuneSight

## Evidence Production Contract Rectification Architecture

### WP-005.0

---

## Document Authority

This document is the governing rectification architecture for [WP-005 Evidence Implementation](WP-005-evidence-implementation.md). It preserves WP-005 as the permanent Evidence implementation Work Package and does not replace, renumber or reinterpret it.

This architecture derives from the [Engineering Constitution](../02-engineering-constitution.md), [Engineering Standards](../engineering-standards/README.md), [Engineering Blueprint](../04-engineering-blueprint.md), ratified [Engineering Architecture Bible](<../05 engineering-architecture-bible/README.md>), its [Evidence chapter](<../05 engineering-architecture-bible/03-evidence.md>), [Cross-Domain Engineering Contracts](<../05 engineering-architecture-bible/00-cross-domain-engineering-contracts.md>), [Production Contract](../06-production-contract.md) and WP-005.

WP-005.0 rectifies bounded original WP-005 and Production Contract gaps. It does not reconstruct the Evidence architecture, alter Engineering Domain ownership or establish runtime implementation.

## Architectural Purpose

WP-005.0 shall establish the minimum production contract required for TuneSight to state truthfully whether authoritative Engineering Evidence has been established.

The rectification is limited to:

1. explicit governed Evidence processing outcomes;
2. truthful unsupported and invalid source handling;
3. visible governed Evidence processing failure;
4. visible governed authoritative Evidence persistence failure;
5. a durable source and Evidence processing lifecycle;
6. minimum original-WP-005 source and transformation provenance;
7. persistence, reload and failure validation; and
8. focused completion-quality rectification only where required.

Evidence remains responsible for validated observations, measurements, events, conditions, anomalies, Evidence qualification and source traceability. It shall not infer causes, create reusable Knowledge, own Correlation, construct Explanation, produce Decision guidance or control Presentation.

## Permanent Engineering Distinctions

WP-005.0 preserves the following distinctions permanently:

- raw source storage is not Evidence establishment;
- source recognition is not source validity;
- successful translation is not Evidence derivation;
- successful in-memory computation is not authoritative Evidence;
- Evidence derivation is not Evidence persistence;
- Evidence persistence is not downstream interpretation;
- upload success is not analysis success;
- retry is not new engineering truth; and
- historical Evidence is not current Evidence merely because it remains persisted.

## Governed Terminal Outcome Model

Every completed Evidence processing lifecycle governed by WP-005.0 shall terminate in exactly one of these outcomes:

- `evidence_established`
- `unsupported_source`
- `invalid_or_incomplete_source`
- `processing_failed`
- `persistence_failed`

These are engineering states. They are not cosmetic Presentation labels.

Every outcome shall preserve, as applicable:

- the durable source or log identity;
- the processing stage reached;
- a governed reason code;
- raw-source availability;
- Evidence availability;
- retry eligibility;
- downstream-consumption permission; and
- a truthful consumer-safe description.

The outcome contract shall be discriminated. It shall reject contradictory combinations and shall not be represented by a success Boolean.

### Evidence Established

`evidence_established` shall apply only when authoritative Engineering Evidence has been durably persisted and is available to authorised downstream consumers and reload.

Evidence is not authoritative merely because it was successfully computed in memory.

### Unsupported Source

`unsupported_source` shall apply when the source cannot be responsibly classified for an implemented authoritative translation path, including a recognised format whose translator is not implemented for authoritative Evidence production.

An unsupported result shall preserve a successfully stored raw source and durable source identity. It shall not create successful-looking empty Evidence, PullWindows, events, verdicts or downstream authority.

No new logger support or logger-precedence architecture is authorised by this outcome.

### Invalid or Incomplete Source

`invalid_or_incomplete_source` shall apply when a supported source fails the minimum preconditions required for the requested Evidence operation. These preconditions may include usable rows, required source headers, required core channels, finite observations, sequence integrity and sufficient source-aligned samples.

The source may remain a valid stored artefact while remaining insufficient to establish the requested Engineering Evidence.

### Processing Failed

`processing_failed` shall apply when supported, sufficiently valid source material cannot complete translation validation or Evidence derivation because processing fails.

No partial or empty analysis shall become authoritative. Successfully stored raw material shall remain available for governed deterministic retry.

### Persistence Failed

`persistence_failed` shall apply when Evidence was computed but could not become durably authoritative.

The in-memory result shall remain transient and unavailable to downstream Evidence consumers. The raw source remains truthful if it was stored successfully. Reload shall not claim that Evidence was established.

## Processing Stages

The terminal outcomes shall remain distinct from the processing stages that lead to them. The owner contract shall define at least:

- `source_registration`
- `raw_source_storage`
- `source_classification`
- `translation_validation`
- `evidence_derivation`
- `evidence_persistence`

Stage identity exists to locate the truthful lifecycle state. Reaching a stage does not imply completion of that stage or Evidence authority.

## Source and Evidence Lifecycle

Raw source persistence and Evidence establishment are separate engineering facts within one staged processing lifecycle:

```text
Source or log identity registered
    |
    v
Raw source stored
    |
    v
Source classified
    |
    v
Translation validated
    |
    v
Evidence derived
    |
    v
Evidence persisted
    |
    v
Evidence established
```

Truthful partial states include:

- raw source stored and source unsupported;
- raw source stored and source invalid or incomplete;
- raw source stored and processing failed;
- raw source stored, Evidence computed and Evidence persistence failed; and
- raw source stored and authoritative Evidence established.

These states shall not be collapsed into generic upload success or failure.

The lifecycle does not require a distributed transaction. Each completed stage shall preserve the truthful state of earlier independently meaningful stages. Safe compensation may remove an unreferenced raw object when durable source identity registration fails, but compensation shall never erase authoritative Evidence.

## Durable Processing State

The processing outcome shall remain durable across navigation and reload. Console output, an in-memory value or a transient redirect parameter cannot constitute the authoritative processing state.

WP-005.0 is architecturally permitted to introduce a narrowly bounded durable processing-state mechanism associated with the source or log lifecycle if the existing schema cannot represent the required state truthfully.

Any schema migration requires separate review and implementation authority within WP-005.0.3. This architecture creates no migration and changes no schema.

## Authoritative Replacement Principle

Previously authoritative persisted Evidence shall never be destroyed before replacement Evidence has successfully become authoritative.

```text
Existing authoritative Evidence remains authoritative
    |
    +-- new Evidence computed
    +-- new Evidence persisted successfully
    +-- replacement authority established
    |
    v
Previous Evidence may then be retired or superseded
```

A failed replacement shall not destroy, weaken or silently displace previously authoritative Evidence. Delete-before-insert replacement is prohibited where deletion could remove the only authoritative Evidence before the replacement is established.

Retirement or supersession semantics shall be bounded by the durable persistence contract and shall preserve historical truth.

## Retry Semantics

WP-005.0 shall:

- preserve the durable source or log identity;
- permit deterministic reprocessing from the stored raw source;
- preserve previously authoritative Evidence during failed retry;
- ensure retry cannot convert a failed state into authority without successful persistence; and
- avoid full Analysis Snapshot or general processing-attempt history architecture.

Broader processing-attempt identity, version history and Analysis Snapshot architecture require separate authority.

## Legacy Records

Existing records without explicit processing outcome or method identity shall remain truthfully identifiable as legacy where necessary.

The rectification shall not retroactively manufacture:

- `evidence_established`;
- an analysis version;
- a translator version;
- a processing outcome; or
- provenance that cannot be demonstrated.

Historical persisted Evidence may remain consumable under its existing semantics when its authority can be demonstrated. Absence of explicit modern metadata shall remain visible and shall not be silently inferred away.

## Minimum Source and Transformation Provenance

The authoritative Evidence envelope shall preserve the minimum provenance required by original WP-005, including as applicable:

- source or log identity;
- selected logger or platform;
- logger classification confidence where produced;
- detected source headers;
- raw source row count;
- translated usable-row count;
- missing core-channel identities;
- the known translation or normalisation basis;
- processing outcome;
- exact source-aligned PullWindow ranges;
- exact source-aligned event ranges; and
- supporting channel identities.

This bounded provenance does not require:

- a source digest;
- a complete method-version graph;
- Analysis Snapshot identity;
- Run identity;
- Gear Evidence;
- GearSegment identity; or
- ShiftEvent identity.

Those capabilities require separately ratified architecture where applicable.

## Failure Ownership

### Infrastructure and API Boundary

Owns authentication, request integrity, vehicle ownership and transport failure. It shall not classify Engineering Evidence.

### Logger Translation Boundary

Owns source recognition, translator capability and translation metadata. Recognition shall not imply usable Evidence.

### Evidence Domain

Owns minimum Evidence preconditions, validated observations, derivation failure, Evidence qualification and the authoritative Evidence outcome.

### Source Persistence Boundary

Owns durable source identity and raw-source storage state.

### Evidence Persistence Boundary

Owns durable Evidence storage, replacement safety and persistence failure.

### Presentation

Communicates the owner-domain outcome without inferring, reclassifying or concealing it.

### Downstream Consumers

Correlation, Explanation, Decision and Presentation may consume established Evidence according to their own contracts. They shall not treat unsupported, invalid, processing-failed or persistence-failed outcomes as established Evidence.

## Failure Atomicity

For every partial processing state, the implementation shall identify:

- what remains persisted;
- what is authoritative;
- what is not authoritative;
- what the user may truthfully be told;
- whether retry is permitted; and
- whether downstream consumption is permitted.

At minimum:

1. Raw storage without durable source identity establishes no consumable Evidence and requires safe reconciliation or compensation.
2. Stored raw source with unsupported translation preserves source authority but establishes no Evidence.
3. Stored raw source with derivation failure preserves source authority but establishes no Evidence.
4. Computed Evidence with persistence failure remains non-authoritative and cannot replace prior authoritative Evidence.
5. Only successful durable Evidence persistence may establish `evidence_established`.

## Downstream Compatibility

The rectification shall preserve successful existing `engine_v2` semantics. Existing successful analyses, PullWindows, events, Telemetry Workspace, Correlation and other consumers shall not change engineering meaning merely because failure outcomes become explicit.

Existing persisted records may be consumed under a truthfully declared legacy contract when their persisted authority is demonstrable. Missing or failed Evidence shall never be reinterpreted as healthy Evidence.

Any Presentation adaptation shall remain a bounded consumer of the governed outcome. Presentation shall not own outcome derivation.

## Static-Quality Boundary

WP-005.0 shall not become a broad historical lint-cleanup programme.

The implementation shall satisfy these rules:

- no new lint debt;
- newly created rectification files are clean;
- directly modified lifecycle and Evidence code is clean where safely achievable;
- bounded behaviour-neutral mechanical debt may be corrected;
- unrelated functional refactors remain outside scope; and
- untouched historical debt may receive an explicit Founder-accepted disposition.

Tune-profile, fueling and root-cause contracts shall not be altered merely to obtain unrelated lint purity.

## Parent WP-005 Completion Gate

WP-005 shall remain incomplete until all of the following are established and validated:

1. Supported valid source produces authoritative persisted Evidence.
2. Persisted Evidence survives reload without semantic change.
3. Unsupported source produces an explicit truthful durable outcome.
4. Invalid or incomplete supported source produces an explicit truthful durable outcome.
5. Evidence derivation failure produces an explicit truthful failure.
6. Evidence persistence failure produces a visible governed failure.
7. Failed replacement cannot destroy previously authoritative Evidence.
8. Raw-source state and Evidence state remain independently truthful.
9. Original WP-005 source and transformation provenance is sufficiently explicit.
10. Failure outcomes block Evidence-dependent downstream consumption.
11. Existing successful `engine_v2` semantics remain preserved.
12. Relevant WP-005 tests pass.
13. Relevant cross-domain regressions pass.
14. TypeScript passes.
15. The accepted static-quality disposition is satisfied.
16. Known unrelated repository failures are reported truthfully.
17. Founder Validation passes.
18. Governance records completion.
19. Required completion ratification is recorded.

Architecture ratification of WP-005.0 does not satisfy this completion gate and does not declare WP-005 complete.
