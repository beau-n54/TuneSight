# WP-004.3.5 — Applicability Evidence Collection and Proposal Construction

Status: locally implemented domain-engine slice; authority decision remains pending.

## Purpose

This work package constructs immutable technical proposals for a relationship between one exact Definition Set Revision and one exact Engineering Binary. It collects evidence and describes admission blockers. It does not approve, admit, publish, persist, or make a relationship authoritative.

## Evidence collection

Internal binary identity collection is bounded by binary size, credible-identifier vocabulary size, and observation count. Callers supply a controlled vocabulary derived from a candidate relationship or governed identity source. The collector searches only exact ASCII and exact hex-encoded representations, preserves every offset and duplicate occurrence, and rejects unrelated printable strings. It deliberately has no best-marker result.

XDF evidence binds the canonical Definition Set Revision to its exact XDF Source Artifact digest and byte length. Version, path, filename, explicit header identities, family association, definition/conflict counts, provenance gaps, and duplicate-source relationships remain evidence. Path and filename are discovery metadata, not authority.

Engineering Binary evidence binds the exact SHA-256 and extracted byte length, container resolution, all internal observations, Stock Variant lookup result, candidate relationship provenance, and provisional source role. `stock_original` and `mapswitch` are source designations only; exact stock authenticity remains a Stock Variant Knowledge question.

## Multi-marker and duplicate-source model

Multiple credible internal markers coexist as observations with independent encoding, offsets, confidence, and reason. Ambiguity is assessed but no marker is selected. One XDF Source Artifact may participate in multiple contextual Definition Set relationships; identical bytes are not falsely represented as different artifacts, and per-ROM candidates are not deduplicated away.

## Proposal identity and assessment

The proposal identity is a domain-separated digest of the exact Definition Set Revision, XDF Source Artifact, and Engineering Binary digest. Its revision is a separate domain digest of engineering-significant evidence. Presentation-only filenames and paths are retained in the package but excluded from revision identity. Material evidence changes create a new revision.

Assessment codes describe strong exact identity correspondence, identity ambiguity, Stock Variant conflict, missing source provenance, a missing candidate binary, or direct technical conflict. Every assessment has `admissionReady: false` and `authorityDecision: required`; every constructed proposal has `authorityDecision: pending`.

## BMW development programme

The developer/test-bounded traversal derives candidate relationships only from exact current repository co-location and matching candidate stems. It preserves every original/stock and MapSwitch binary independently, emits `no_responsible_candidate_binary` for unpaired sets, and generates no static proposal files. Duplicate XDF digest groups are counted without collapsing relationship candidates.

## Exclusions and integrity boundary

- No admission, authorised decision, publication, receipt, registry, or production Stock Variant mutation.
- No runtime, Tune upload, Analysis, Calibration Context, dashboard, Workshop, persistence, Supabase, or Native Vehicle Data integration.
- No XDF equation execution, engineering-value conversion, stock/modified comparison, recommendations, or editor work.
- No XDF/BIN source mutation, network access, arbitrary filesystem traversal, or arbitrary code execution.
- The 133 representation-conflicted Definitions remain conflicted and cannot gain calibrated-intent authority from a proposal.

## Validation and completion condition

Completion requires deterministic identity/proposal tests; N54, B58 Gen1/Gen2, combined N55/S55/N13, N55E, S58, and S63 fixtures; complete 99-set/212-relationship coverage; existing applicability, XDF, extraction, Stock Variant, Calibration Knowledge, Engineering Binary, and ROM identity regressions; TypeScript; focused ESLint; and whitespace validation. The slice completes only with authority still absent.
