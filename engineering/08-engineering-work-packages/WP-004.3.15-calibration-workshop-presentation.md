## Definition Knowledge binding foundation

Workshop Definition Information is separated permanently into two layers. Layer A is mandatory structural information derived from qualified Dataset/XDF/comparison Evidence: identities and revisions, shape and dimensions, units and axes, supplied description/category, availability and comparison state, roles, ROM Layout, Dataset/source/equation provenance, and limitations. Layer B is optional semantic information admitted only through governed exact Definition identities/revisions or explicit applicability relationships.

Semantic outcomes are `exact`, `partial`, `ambiguous`, `conflict`, and `unavailable`. Title resemblance is not binding Evidence. Engineering-system classification, intent search, parameter education, semantic axis meaning, related calibrations, and telemetry relationships require qualified assertion-scoped Knowledge with revision, authority, provenance, and limitations. Missing Knowledge remains `Unclassified` and “Engineering interpretation not yet available.” Literal search and `All Maps` remain independent and permanent.

The binding contract is BMW-family-neutral. It contains no N54-specific semantic branch and can consume future qualified Knowledge for N54, N55, N13, S55, B58 generations, S58, S63, and other families without changing the presentation contract. It does not query a Knowledge registry from React and does not author Knowledge.
