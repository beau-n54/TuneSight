# WP-004.3.6 — Applicability Authority Assessment and Decision

Status: bounded domain architecture; no relationship is admitted or published.

## Assessment boundary

An Applicability Authority Assessment binds one exact Applicability Proposal revision and deterministically describes what its Evidence supports. It records explicit satisfied, unsatisfied, conflicting, and not-applicable requirements. Assessment never impersonates a reviewer and never converts proposal strength into approval.

Outcomes distinguish reviewable exact-applicability Evidence, more Evidence required, technical conflict, unresolved source authority, Stock Variant conflict, no responsible candidate, and invalid input. Reviewability is separate from acceptance eligibility, and both remain separate from an authorised decision.

## Authority pathways

The contract represents governed pathways for explicit source authority, authoritative Stock Variant relationships, and governed Evidence review. These are capability pathways rather than automatic ratification rules. Decision authority preserves a stable authority identity, authority class, revision, provenance, permitted outcomes, and accepted pathways; no personal name is embedded in domain logic.

## Decision states and explicit authority

An authorised reviewer may explicitly accept exact applicability, reject the relationship, record conflict, require more Evidence, or defer. More-Evidence and defer remain semantically distinct. Acceptance requires an eligible review assessment plus a pathway accepted by the reviewer scope. Proposal existence, marker correspondence, filenames, `exact_candidate`, or Evidence quantity cannot accept a relationship.

Hard exact-identity contradictions and Stock Variant conflicts fail closed for acceptance. Conflict and unresolved Evidence remain in non-accepting records. Every Decision Record retains accepted and rejected/overridden Evidence references, rationale, unresolved conditions, explicit canonical decision time, and authority provenance.

## Identity and lineage

Decision identity binds the exact proposal relationship, assessment lineage, and authority identity. Revision identity includes proposal/assessment revisions, outcome, authority, rationale, Evidence dispositions, unresolved conditions, supplied decision time, and predecessor. Correction is append-only: a later revision references the immutable prior decision and must preserve stable lineage identity.

## Publication exclusion

Every Decision Record states `not_published`. There is no publication instruction, registry mutation, persistence, runtime lookup, endpoint, or UI surface. A future separately authorised slice may transform an acceptable Decision Record into a deterministic publication instruction. Until then, the calibrated-intent gate remains unchanged and Decision Records cannot qualify runtime applicability.

## Conflict and cohort proofs

Tests cover coherent and wrong-ROM N54, current/Legacy and original/MapSwitch independence, B58 ambiguity and missing provenance, combined N55/S55/N13 Stock/platform conflict, duplicate XDF source independence, four-unpaired-set failure, and preservation of the 133 contained representation conflicts.

## Completion condition

Completion requires deterministic assessment/decision tests, relevant proposal/applicability/XDF/Stock/Knowledge/Binary/ROM regressions, TypeScript, focused ESLint, whitespace validation, complete diff review, and confirmation that no admission, publication, runtime, equation, Supabase, Native Vehicle Data, or WP-005.1 surface changed.
