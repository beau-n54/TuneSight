## Presentation Domain Definition

The Presentation architectural layer communicates existing engineering truth. It may own:

- information and presentation hierarchy;
- reading order and report flow;
- evidence grouping and visual prominence;
- professional engineering language;
- confidence and provenance communication;
- information density and progressive disclosure;
- interaction expectations;
- consistent presentation contracts.

Presentation does not own Vehicle Identity, Knowledge, Runtime Knowledge, binary parsing, EngineeringBinary, comparison qualification or calculations, evidence generation or weighting, event detection, cause ranking, Reasoning, Cross Reference logic, confidence calculations, recommendation generation, checksum verification, Software Version or Calibration ID.

Presentation may format, organise and expose owner-domain outputs. It shall preserve their meaning, qualification, uncertainty, conflicts, limitations and provenance.

## Core Presentation Objective

Every major engineering surface shall answer, in this cognitive order:

1. What does TuneSight know?
2. Why does TuneSight know it?
3. What should the engineer inspect next?

The first question communicates the conclusion. The second preserves its evidence and reasoning. The third communicates an existing recommendation or inspection direction without generating one in Presentation.

## Engineering Information Hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Engineering Conclusions

Tier 1 provides immediate engineering understanding and receives the highest prominence. It may contain Vehicle Identity, the overall engineering summary, Primary Cause, critical warnings, overall analysis confidence and the highest-priority inspection direction when those outputs exist.

### Tier 2 — Engineering Evidence

Tier 2 explains why a conclusion exists. It may contain telemetry, observed events, Comparison results, Knowledge qualification, evidence summaries, event timing, supporting metrics, modified-region evidence and corroborating signals. Evidence shall remain factual, traceable and accessible.

### Tier 3 — Engineering Interpretation

Tier 3 expands the explanation without competing with Tier 1. It may contain Alternative Causes, Rejected Causes, event-specific interpretation, Tune Notes, Cross Reference, calibration areas, hardware relationships and existing inspection guidance.

### Tier 4 — Engineering Metadata and Provenance

Tier 4 preserves technical traceability without dominating the engineering decision. It may contain Source Container, Engineering Binary, file size, upload time, hashes, runtime identifiers, storage references, processing path, Knowledge source, analysis engine and Comparison source.

Information belongs to a tier according to its engineering purpose, not its current screen location.

## Engineering Evidence Chain

Every displayed engineering conclusion shall remain traceable through:

```text
Observation
    |
    v
Evidence
    |
    v
Reasoning
    |
    v
Confidence
    |
    v
Recommendation
```

Presentation may condense this chain visually but shall not break it logically. An engineer shall be able to move from a conclusion to its supporting evidence and source. Missing links shall be presented truthfully and shall not be reconstructed by Presentation.

## Cause Prioritisation Presentation

### Primary Cause

The highest-ranked supported diagnosis shall dominate cause presentation. Where supplied by the owner contract, it shall show the cause name, confidence, supporting evidence, contradictions or limitations and recommended inspection path.

### Alternative Causes

Supported lower-ranked hypotheses shall remain available and individually traceable. They shall not compete visually with the Primary Cause.

### Rejected Causes

Evaluated hypotheses contradicted, unsupported or displaced by stronger evidence shall remain visible as part of the engineering record. Where the owner output supplies a reason, Presentation shall state why the cause was rejected.

Presentation shall not create causes, alter ranks, recalculate confidence or convert an alternative or rejected cause into a Primary Cause.

## Engineering Note Hierarchy

### Global Tune Notes

Global Tune Notes apply to the complete tune, analysis, vehicle state or multiple events. They may communicate recurring behaviour, global calibration concerns, repeated safety observations or broad hardware context when those outputs exist.

### Event-Specific Notes

Event-specific notes apply to one detected event, pull region, operating condition or diagnostic cause.

Presentation may reduce repeated wording by referencing a global note from related events. It shall not suppress unique event evidence or imply that a global note applies to an event unless the underlying output establishes that relationship.

## Confidence Presentation Contract

Confidence is an engineering assertion, not decoration. Presentation shall preserve the distinction between:

- overall analysis confidence;
- diagnosis confidence;
- event confidence;
- pull quality.

These concepts shall not be merged or substituted for one another.

Where owner outputs expose contributors, Presentation may communicate qualified stock Knowledge, telemetry completeness, pull quality, corroborating signals, evidence agreement, contradictory evidence, missing channels, incomplete Comparison and uncertain Knowledge qualification. It shall not infer contributors that the runtime does not expose.

Where only a score exists, Presentation shall label its scope truthfully and shall not invent an explanation, verification state or confidence breakdown. Confidence shall never be displayed as verification.

## Telemetry Graph Intelligence Architecture

Future graph presentation shall aim for accurate and smooth trace rendering, clean line treatment, professional calibration-software appearance, readable multi-channel Comparison, clear pull boundaries and event regions, understandable target-versus-actual relationships, legible axes and units, useful inspection states, reduced clutter, consistent channel names and event-to-evidence traceability.

The graph contract distinguishes:

- raw telemetry values;
- rendered visual interpolation;
- event annotations;
- engineering interpretation overlays.

Raw values remain authoritative. Rendering interpolation shall not alter samples, event boundaries, peaks, minima or calculations. An annotation or overlay shall remain attributable to its owner-domain output and visually distinct from measured telemetry.

No graph implementation is authorised by WP-011.0.

## Provenance Presentation Contract

Presentation shall preserve the following provenance chain where the corresponding outputs exist:

```text
Source Container

    v

    |
    v

    |
    v
Knowledge Qualification
    |
    v
Comparison
    |
    v
Analysis Engine
    |
    v
Reasoning
    |
    v

```

The original uploaded artefact, resolved Engineering Binary, derived Vehicle Identity, qualified Knowledge, Comparison evidence, analysis output and recommendation are separate records and shall not be visually conflated.

For example, an existing supported analysis may identify the Source Container as DTF, resolve an MG1 full-image Engineering Binary and present a `Family Match` Knowledge outcome. Such values are examples of current output, not hard-coded architecture requirements. Numeric Comparison results shall be shown only when supplied by the active analysis record.

## Professional Engineering Language Standard

Presentation language shall be objective, professional, technically precise, evidence-based, concise, truthful and appropriately cautious. It shall avoid marketing exaggeration, generic software language and unsupported certainty.

Preferred patterns include:

- “Evidence indicates…”
- “The strongest supported cause is…”
- “This hypothesis is supported by…”
- “This cause was rejected because…”
- “The available telemetry does not establish…”
- “Comparison evidence confirms…”
- “Knowledge qualification supports…”
- “Further inspection should begin with…”

Prohibited patterns include unsupported claims such as “guaranteed cause”, “definitely broken”, “perfect result”, “AI thinks”, “smart diagnosis”, “amazing confidence” or “everything looks good”.

Wording shall reflect the certainty, scope and qualification of the originating engineering output.

## Information Density and Progressive Disclosure

Presentation shall apply four visual priorities:

1. Immediate engineering decision.
2. Evidence supporting that decision.
3. Supporting interpretation and alternatives.
4. Metadata and deep technical provenance.

Reducing prominence shall not delete, permanently hide or suppress engineering evidence. Progressive disclosure may manage density, but critical warnings, the Primary Cause, confidence limitations and major contradictions shall not depend on optional interaction to become known.

## Presentation Consistency

The same presentation contracts apply across N54, B58 Gen1, B58TU and future BMW platforms; BIN and supported DTF Engineering Binary inputs; stock, modified and Comparison uploads; log analysis; Tune History; and refreshed analysis.

Platforms and workflows may produce different engineering evidence. Presentation shall represent those differences through owner-domain outputs rather than platform-specific visual truth. Platform-specific presentation logic is justified only when the engineering output or interaction requirement genuinely differs.

## Accessibility and Usability Principles

Presentation shall provide:

- readable typography and clear labels;
- explicit units;
- colour-independent meaning;
- no warning or cause status conveyed by colour alone;
- keyboard-accessible interaction where interaction exists;
- understandable confidence labels;
- accessible graph inspection;
- mobile readability;
- appropriate desktop engineering density;
- high-information layouts without visual congestion.

Accessibility shall preserve engineering depth and qualification.

## Presentation Principles

### EP-001 — Presentation Clarifies Engineering Truth

Presentation shall clarify engineering truth and shall not replace it.

### EP-002 — Visual Hierarchy Follows Engineering Importance

Prominence shall reflect engineering consequence, qualification and confidence.

### EP-003 — Evidence Precedes Explanation

A conclusion shall remain connected to supporting Evidence.

### EP-004 — Confidence Remains Justified

Confidence shall be presented with its scope, limits and available contributors.

### EP-005 — Provenance Remains Traceable

Every conclusion shall remain traceable to its engineering origin.

### EP-006 — Supporting Evidence Remains Accessible

Reduced prominence shall not remove Evidence.

### EP-007 — Rejected Hypotheses Remain Visible

Rejected Causes remain part of the engineering record.

### EP-008 — Cognitive Load May Decrease

Engineering depth shall not decrease.

### EP-009 — Presentation Does Not Manufacture Certainty

Visual design and language shall not imply stronger confidence or verification than the originating output.

### EP-010 — Original Artefacts Remain Distinct

The uploaded Source Container, resolved Engineering Binary and derived engineering outputs shall never be visually conflated.

## Presentation Contracts

These contracts govern future presentation implementation. They do not define or authorise new runtime models.

### Engineering Summary Contract

When available, present the primary engineering conclusion, overall confidence, major limitations and next inspection direction.

### Diagnostic Cause Contract

When available, present cause status, rank, confidence, Evidence, contradictions and recommended inspection path.

### Event Contract

When available, present event name, timing or region, severity or priority, event confidence, supporting channels and event-specific notes. Absence of a field shall remain explicit rather than inferred.

### Confidence Contract

Preserve the distinct scope and meaning of overall, diagnosis and event confidence and pull quality.

### Provenance Contract

Preserve the full available processing chain and distinguish source artefacts from derived outputs.

### Graph Contract

Preserve raw numerical truth, units, event boundaries and Evidence traceability.

### Notes Contract

Distinguish Global Tune Notes from event-specific notes while preserving unique Evidence.

## Failure and Uncertainty Presentation

Unknown, unavailable, unsupported, invalid, conflicting and not implemented are distinct states.

Examples include unknown Software Version, unknown Calibration ID, checksum verification not implemented, incomplete telemetry, unavailable stock reference, `Family Match` rather than exact match, unsupported container, unresolved Engineering Binary, insufficient Comparison evidence and low-quality pull.

Presentation shall use the exact state supplied by the owner contract or a faithful user-facing equivalent. It shall not convert missing Knowledge into inferred certainty. “Not implemented” shall not be presented as “not detected”; “unavailable” shall not be presented as “unknown”; candidate or family evidence shall not be presented as verified exact identity.
