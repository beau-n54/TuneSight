# TuneSight Engineering Standards

Engineering Standards define stable, assessable and implementation-independent engineering obligations. They occupy the permanent governance layer between the [Engineering Principles](../03-engineering-principles.md) and the [Engineering Blueprint](../04-engineering-blueprint.md).

Every TuneSight Engineering Standard derives its authority from [TS-STD-000](TS-STD-000-engineering-standards-framework.md) and shall remain conformant to it.

## Authority Order

```text
Founder Vision
    ↓
Engineering Manifesto
    ↓
Engineering Constitution
    ↓
Engineering Principles
    ↓
Engineering Standards
    ↓
Engineering Blueprint
    ↓
Engineering Architecture Bible
    ↓
Production Contract
    ↓
Work Packages
    ↓
Implementation Contracts
    ↓
Runtime Implementation
```

## Standards Register

| Identifier | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TS-STD-000 | [Engineering Standards Framework](TS-STD-000-engineering-standards-framework.md) | Founder Ratified |
| TS-STD-001 | Engineering Truth Standard | Engineering Hold; not authorised, drafted or ratified |

## Boundary

Standards define obligations. They do not define Engineering Domains, Architecture, Work Packages, implementation, runtime behaviour, Engineering Knowledge, Engineering Evidence or production authority.
