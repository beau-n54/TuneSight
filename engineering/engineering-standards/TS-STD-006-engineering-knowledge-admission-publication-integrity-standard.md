# TS-STD-006

## Engineering Knowledge Admission and Publication Integrity Standard

## 1. Purpose

This Standard defines the permanent, implementation-independent obligations governing Engineering Knowledge Admission and Publication.

It ensures that only an exact, sufficiently governed and explicitly authorised Knowledge revision may cross the Admission boundary and that Publication makes precisely that revision canonical through a deterministic, evidentiary and historically reproducible process.

This Standard governs lifecycle integrity without defining Engineering Truth, Evidence, Qualification, Identity, semantic meaning or Knowledge content.

## 2. Scope

This Standard applies to Candidate Knowledge, Admission Proposals, proposed Knowledge revisions, Admission assessments, policies and Decisions, publication eligibility, Publication Instructions and Sources, Publication Execution, Registry records and snapshots, Publication Receipts, correction, supersession, withdrawal, historical Publication and canonical Knowledge consumption.

It is implementation-independent, architecture-independent, manufacturer-neutral, platform-neutral, storage-neutral, schema-neutral and historically reproducible.

## 3. Authority and Framework Conformance

TS-STD-006 derives authority from the Founder Vision, Engineering Manifesto, Engineering Constitution, Engineering Principles and TS-STD-000.

Ratification establishes normative governance authority only. It does not establish conformance, admissibility, Publication, truth or production readiness for any existing engineering material and authorises no production Knowledge.

```text

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
```

No stage may substitute for another. Structural validity is not Admission. Qualification is not Admission. Admission is not Publication. Publication eligibility is not Publication. A Publication Instruction is not execution. Execution is not evidentiary proof. Storage is not Registry authority. Registry membership is not Engineering Truth. Historical Knowledge is not necessarily current Knowledge.

## 6. Engineering Knowledge Admission Integrity Principle

Engineering Knowledge Admission and Publication shall always remain subordinate to engineering reality.

Admission and Publication exist to faithfully preserve governed Engineering Knowledge.

Engineering Knowledge shall never be admitted, published, preserved or retained merely to protect historical Admission Decisions, implementation convenience, publication history, Registry state or consumer expectation where governed engineering reality demonstrates that the represented Knowledge no longer faithfully reflects engineering understanding.

Where governed Engineering Truth, Engineering Evidence, Qualification, Identity or Semantic Integrity demonstrates that existing Engineering Knowledge no longer faithfully represents engineering reality, Admission and Publication shall preserve history while evolving current canonical Knowledge only through governed processes.

Historical Engineering Knowledge shall remain historically reproducible.

Current Engineering Knowledge shall faithfully represent the best governed engineering understanding available within its declared scope.

## 7. Engineering Canonical Knowledge Principle

Canonical Engineering Knowledge shall always remain subordinate to engineering reality.

Canonical Publication exists to faithfully preserve governed engineering understanding.

Canonical Engineering Knowledge shall never be admitted, published, preserved or retained merely to protect historical Admission Decisions, historical Publication state, Registry state, institutional precedent, implementation convenience or consumer expectation where governed engineering reality demonstrates that the represented Knowledge no longer faithfully reflects engineering understanding.

Where governed Engineering Truth, Engineering Evidence, Engineering Qualification, Engineering Identity or Engineering Semantic Integrity demonstrate that existing canonical Engineering Knowledge no longer faithfully represents engineering reality, canonical Knowledge shall evolve only through governed Admission, correction, supersession or withdrawal while preserving complete historical reproducibility.

Historical Engineering Knowledge shall remain historically reproducible.

Current Engineering Knowledge shall faithfully represent the best governed engineering understanding available within its declared scope.

**Engineering Knowledge Principle:** Engineering Knowledge does not protect historical publication. Engineering Knowledge protects the faithful governed preservation of engineering understanding. Where engineering reality evolves, Engineering Knowledge shall evolve. Engineering reality shall always remain the higher authority.

## 8. Definitions

- **Candidate Knowledge:** Governed material considered as potential canonical Knowledge but not accepted through Admission.
- **Admission Proposal:** An immutable, independently identified request to assess one exact proposed Knowledge revision for one declared purpose.
- **Proposed Knowledge Revision:** The exact immutable Knowledge revision presented for Admission, distinct from its proposal and every other revision.
- **Qualified Admission Assessment:** The governed collection of exact assessment results required by applicable policy; assessment does not authorise Admission.
- **Authorised Admission Decision:** An immutable decision under an exact valid Authority mandate and policy concerning one exact proposed revision.
- **Admitted Knowledge Revision:** One exact proposed revision accepted for its declared canonical purpose; Admission does not prove Publication.
- **Publication Instruction:** An immutable, independently identified instruction for one exact Publication operation and admitted revision.
- **Publication Execution:** The bounded attempt to perform the exact instructed operation.
- **Published Canonical Knowledge:** An admitted revision whose successful governed Publication is established through sufficient evidence.
- **Registry Record:** An exact governed reference to a published canonical entity and revision; recording does not establish Truth or Admission.
- **Registry Snapshot:** An immutable identity for one exact governed Registry state and record set.
- **Publication Receipt:** An immutable evidentiary record proving or qualifying one exact Publication Execution result.
- **Current Knowledge:** Published canonical Knowledge authorised for current use within declared scope and lifecycle.
- **Historical Knowledge:** A prior Knowledge state preserved against the exact governance and Publication context applicable to it.
- **Withdrawn Knowledge:** Previously admitted or published Knowledge prohibited from current governed use through authorised withdrawal.
- **Superseded Knowledge:** Knowledge replaced for some or all current uses by an exact successor while remaining historically preserved.

## 9. Permanent Distinctions

Engineering material is not Candidate Knowledge. Candidate Knowledge is not Admitted Knowledge. A proposal is not its proposed revision. Structural validity is not Qualification. Qualification is not Admission. Assessment is not a Decision. Authority is not Admission. Publication eligibility is not Admission. Admission is not Publication. A Publication Instruction is not Publication Execution. Execution is not proof. Persistence success is not canonical Publication. Registry membership is not Engineering Truth. A Registry Snapshot is not a Publication Receipt. A Receipt records an outcome but does not authorise it. Rejection is not non-selection. Deferral is not Rejection. Unresolved is not Conflict. Failed Publication is not rejected Knowledge. Indeterminate Publication is neither success nor proven non-publication. Correction is not supersession. Supersession is not withdrawal. Withdrawal is not deletion. Historical Knowledge is not current Knowledge. Replay is not new Publication. Current policy is not historical policy.

## 10. Permanent Admission Model

```text
Immutable Admission Proposal
    ↓
Structural and Contract Validity
    ↓
Exact Revision and Lineage Binding
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
```

Each stage consumes exact prior outputs without recreating, reinterpreting or strengthening them. Admission determines whether one exact proposed revision is authorised to become canonical under one exact policy and Authority mandate. It is not truth generation, Evidence interpretation, semantic repair or Publication.

## 11. Admission Outcomes

- **Invalid:** Required Admission material is malformed or internally impossible; this is not engineering Rejection.
- **Requires Evidence:** Required governed Evidence is absent, incomplete or insufficient.
- **Requires Authority:** Required Authority is absent, inapplicable, expired, withdrawn or unresolved.
- **Assessment Incomplete:** One or more mandatory assessments remain incomplete.
- **Unresolved:** Available governed material cannot establish one outcome without inference.
- **Conflict:** Applicable Evidence, assessments, identities, semantics, authorities or policies materially disagree.
- **Deferred:** An authorised decision postpones Admission without acceptance or Rejection.
- **Rejected:** The exact proposal is not admitted under applicable policy.
- **Admitted:** One exact proposed revision is authorised for its declared canonical purpose.

## 12. Permanent Publication Model

```text
Admitted Knowledge Revision
    ↓
Exact Authorised Decision Binding
    ↓
Deterministic Canonical Representation
    ↓
Publication Instruction
    ↓
Qualified Publication Source
    ↓
Exact Predecessor Registry Snapshot
    ↓
Atomic Publication Execution
    ↓
Publication Execution Result
    ↓
Resulting Registry Snapshot
    ↓
Evidentiary Publication Receipt
```

Publication preserves the exact admitted revision. It does not reassess Admission or modify Knowledge for implementation, Registry or consumer convenience.

## 13. Publication Outcomes

- **Published:** The exact authorised revision was atomically published and resulting canonical state is proven.
- **Idempotent Replay:** The exact prior successful Publication was replayed without a new Knowledge version or Registry transition.
- **Blocked:** Execution did not begin because a governed precondition was unsatisfied.
- **Failed:** Execution is proven not to have published the governed unit, including proven atomic non-publication.
- **Indeterminate:** Evidence proves neither complete Publication nor complete non-publication.
- **Reconciled:** A later governed result resolves an earlier indeterminate outcome while preserving both states and lineage.

## 16. Correction, Supersession and Withdrawal

Correction, supersession and withdrawal are governed lifecycle operations and never mutate historical Publication. Each preserves exact affected Knowledge, predecessor, operation identity, reason, Evidence, Authority, scope, effective boundary, compatibility, affected Assertions and relationships, new Admission and Publication evidence where current canonical state changes, and complete historical state.

Correction repairs an error while referent continuity remains valid. Supersession replaces Knowledge for current use and may be complete or partial. Withdrawal prohibits current use where continued use would violate applicable obligations.

## 17. Consumer Obligations

Consumers preserve exact Knowledge identity and revision, Assertion and relationship revisions, Admission and Publication lifecycle, current or historical status, Qualification, Evidence, provenance, semantic context, Conflict, applicable Registry Snapshot and exact Receipt references where material.

Consumers do not treat availability as validity, Registry membership as Truth, withdrawn Knowledge as current, storage as Publication, or unresolved Publication as certainty. They do not reconstruct missing Admission evidence, replace historical revisions with current ones, mutate or republish canonical Knowledge.

## 18. Cross-Standard Boundaries

TS-STD-001 governs Engineering Truth. TS-STD-002 governs Evidence. TS-STD-003 governs Qualification and Authority. TS-STD-004 governs identity, revision, provenance and traceability. TS-STD-005 governs preservation of meaning. Admission and Publication consume and preserve these exact governed outputs without assuming their ownership.

## 19. Non-Ownership

TS-STD-006 does not define Engineering Truth, Evidence, Qualification, Authority mandates, Verification, Confidence, universal Identity architecture, semantic meaning, Ontology, Vocabulary, Knowledge content, manufacturer engineering truth, Registry implementation, database technology, persistence, schemas, APIs, publication-engine algorithms, runtime technology, Explanation, Decision or Presentation.

## 20. Conformance

Potential subjects include Admission contracts, proposal constructors, assessment pipelines, policies, Decisions, publication-eligibility assessments, canonical serialization, Publication Instructions and Sources, Execution Results, idempotency mechanisms, Registry contracts and Snapshots, Receipts, lifecycle processes, historical replay and consumers.

Assessments identify exact subject and version, this Standard version, applicable requirement revisions, method, evidence, assessment Authority, findings, exceptions, outcome and lifecycle.

Passing tests may demonstrate Admission and Publication integrity. They cannot establish the engineering correctness of admitted or published Knowledge.

## 21. Expected Conformance Evidence

Expected evidence may include proposal and revision manifests, predecessor and lifecycle references, Evidence-package and assessment identities, policy revisions, Authority mandates, deterministic reconstruction, outcome fixtures, canonical vectors and digests, Instructions, Publication Sources, Snapshots, composite manifests, atomicity, idempotency and retry records, Execution Results, Receipts, Registry membership proof, correction, supersession and withdrawal records, historical reconstruction, consumer audits and negative substitution tests.

## 22. Compatibility and Evolution

The requirements are foundational. Material change to the lifecycle, outcomes, exact revision binding, policy or Authority binding, canonical representation, atomicity, idempotency, Snapshot semantics, Receipt evidence, correction, supersession, withdrawal, historical reproducibility or consumer obligations requires governed compatibility review.

Migration preserves exact historical Admission and Publication meaning. Ratification of a new Standard version does not migrate, reassess, republish or establish conformance for existing Knowledge.

## 23. Permanent Prohibitions

Prohibited conduct includes structural validity represented as Admission; Qualification represented as Admission; Admission represented as Publication; eligibility represented as Admission; instruction represented as execution; execution represented as proven Publication without evidence; storage represented as canonical Publication; Registry membership represented as Engineering Truth; Admission of mutable or unbound revisions; Publication of a different revision; unverified caller substitution; upstream assessment reinterpretation; Publication recalculation of Admission; Authority inferred from confidence, expertise or access; concealed Conflict; erased Rejection; deferred treated as rejected; unresolved coerced into acceptance; withdrawn Knowledge erased; superseded Knowledge rewritten; current policy substituted into history; partial atomic Publication presented as success; retries creating unintended versions; failed Publication treated as Knowledge Rejection; indeterminate Publication treated as certainty; destructive Registry mutation; historical Knowledge presented as current; runtime learning directly mutating canonical Knowledge; AI output independently admitted or published; Publication changing meaning for convenience; and history preserved at the expense of engineering reality.
